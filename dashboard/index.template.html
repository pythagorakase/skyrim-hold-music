The Palette

Provisional Baseline · Listening at Your Pace

The eleven regional styles remain provisional. Listening helps refine them while project development continues. Sean-nós is the current Reach challenger; earlier candidates and saved takes remain under Deferred.

Optional Listening Guide
  1. Keep the recipe and performer arrangement fixed for a comparison. Retain exact prompts, model, lyrics and takes.
  2. Listen to independent takes when convenient; record structure, enjoyment and failures separately.
  3. Blind-compare nearby styles and review which pairs are confused.
  4. Try fresh English lyrics when useful. Winterhold stays wordless; Hjaalmarch instrumental.
  5. Use what you hear to revise a style without waiting for the whole palette to pass.

Listening approval does not block engineering or require all eleven styles to pass before another style can be developed. Changes to the active workshop set are deliberate recipe revisions; your guesses and Keep / Rework reactions remain listening evidence.
